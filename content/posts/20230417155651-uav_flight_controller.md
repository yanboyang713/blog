Flight controller also called Autopilot, or FC for short, stabilizes and controls a [UAV]({{< relref "20230327173543-uav.md" >}}) for semi-autonomous and autonomous flights. It is one of the core components of a UAV. Flight controller primarily consists of a microcontroller, control circuit &amp; a suite of sensors (gyroscope, accelerator, electronic compass, barometer, etc.). It has redundant sensors &amp; power supplies and performs redundant switching in case of failure to ensure flight safety.
