The reliability of [wireless networks]({{< relref "20230404164903-wireless_networks.md" >}}) communication (WNC) is much lower than wired networks. WNC will impact by node overload, routing protocol, weather, obstacle blockage, and many other factors, all those [anomalies]({{< relref "20230407033403-anomalies.md" >}}) cannot be avoided.
