In [UAV]({{< relref "20230327173543-uav.md" >}}) smooth motion planning, the cubic spline method is the most used to fit the path point with corresponding time interval. Using cubic spline and time reallocation mechanism, we can obtain a smooth trajectory that satisfies the dynamic constraints.


## Reference List {#reference-list}

1.  Sun, Y., Zhang, C., Sun, P., &amp; Liu, C. (2020). Safe and smooth motion planning for mecanum-wheeled robot using improved RRT and cubic spline. Arabian Journal for Science and Engineering, 45, 3075-3090.
