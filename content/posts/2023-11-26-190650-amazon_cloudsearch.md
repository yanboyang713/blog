Amazon CloudSearch is a managed service in the [AWS]({{< relref "20230314144515-aws.md" >}}) Cloud that makes it simple and cost-effective to set up, manage, and scale a search solution for your website or application.
Amazon CloudSearch supports 34 languages and popular search features such as highlighting, autocomplete, and geospatial search.
