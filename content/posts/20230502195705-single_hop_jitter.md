Single-hop jitter is a metric that calculates the variation in [Single-hop delay]({{< relref "20230502200932-single_hop_delay.md" >}}). Real-time audio and video applications are very sensitive to jitter [1].

1.  Chen, Y., Farley, T., &amp; Ye, N. (2004). QoS requirements of network applications on the Internet. Information Knowledge Systems Management, 4(1), 55-76.
