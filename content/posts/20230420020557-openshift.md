OpenShift is a family of containerization software products developed by Red Hat. Its flagship product is the OpenShift Container Platform — a hybrid cloud platform as a service built around Linux containers orchestrated and managed by [Kubernetes]({{< relref "20230105185343-kubernetes.md" >}}) on a foundation of Red Hat Enterprise Linux. The family's other products provide this platform through different environments: OKD serves as the community-driven upstream (akin to the way that Fedora is upstream of Red Hat Enterprise Linux), Several deployment methods are available including self-managed, cloud native under ROSA (Red Hat OpenShift Service on AWS), ARO (Azure Red Hat OpenShift) and RHOIC (Red Hat OpenShift on IBM Cloud) on [AWS]({{< relref "20230314144515-aws.md" >}}), [Azure]({{< relref "20230328005951-azure.md" >}}) , and IBM Cloud respectively, OpenShift Online as software as a service, and OpenShift Dedicated as a managed service.
