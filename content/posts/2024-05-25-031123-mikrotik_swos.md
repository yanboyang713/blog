SwOS is an operating system designed specifically for administration of [MikroTik]({{< relref "20230226101927-mikrotik.md" >}}) switch products.

MikroTik’s SwOS does not support [AP Controller (CAPsMAN)]({{< relref "2024-01-28-194857-ap_controller_capsman.md" >}}).
