[Pixlr](https://pixlr.com/) is a cloud-based set of image editing tools and utilities, including a number of photo editors and a [photo sharing service]({{< relref "20230419062654-photo_sharing_service.md" >}}). It was acquired by Autodesk in 2011. The suite is intended from the range of simple to advanced photo editing.[1] It features three subscription plans which include Free, Premium and Creative Pack.

The platform can be used on desktop and also smartphones and tablets.[2] Pixlr is compatible with various image formats such as JPEG, PNG, WEBP, GIF, PSD (Photoshop Document) and PXZ (native Pixlr document format).[3]
