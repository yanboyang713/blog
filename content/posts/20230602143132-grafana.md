This open-source platform for visualization of metrics and analytics provides four built-in dashboards for Kubernetes—Cluster, Node, Pod/Container and Deployment. Kubernetes administrators can create data-rich dashboards in Grafana using the information sourced from [Prometheus]({{< relref "20230602142910-prometheus.md" >}}).
