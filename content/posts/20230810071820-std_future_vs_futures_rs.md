## std::future and futures-rs {#std-future-and-futures-rs}

Rust has two kinds of types commonly referred to as Future:

-   the first is std::future::[Future]({{< relref "20230810071548-future_trait.md" >}}) from [Rust's Standard Library]({{< relref "20230803013733-rust_standard_library.md" >}}).
-   the second is futures::future::Future from the [futures-rs]({{< relref "20230807100446-futures_rs.md" >}}) crate.
