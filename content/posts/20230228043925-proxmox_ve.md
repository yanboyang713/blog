## Introduction {#introduction}

Proxmox VE is a complete, open-source server management platform for enterprise virtualization. Based on [Debian]({{< relref "20230228044717-debian.md" >}}). It tightly integrates the [Kernel Based Virtual Machive (KVM)]({{< relref "2024-07-12-090401-kernel_based_virtual_machive_kvm.md" >}}) hypervisor and [Linux Containers (LXC)]({{< relref "2023-12-03-143424-lxc.md" >}}), software-defined storage and networking functionality (such as,[EVPN]({{< relref "2025-09-28-131926-setting_up_evpn_on_proxmox_sdn.md" >}})), on a single platform. With the integrated web-based user interface you can manage VMs and containers, high availability for clusters, or the integrated disaster recovery tools with ease.
