## Introduction {#introduction}

Proxmox VE is a complete, open-source server management platform for enterprise virtualization. Based on [Debian]({{< relref "20230228044717-debian.md" >}}). It tightly integrates the KVM hypervisor and Linux Containers (LXC), software-defined storage and networking functionality, on a single platform. With the integrated web-based user interface you can manage VMs and containers, high availability for clusters, or the integrated disaster recovery tools with ease.
