Volcano is an enhanced, open-source, cloud-native batch system built on [Kubernetes]({{< relref "20230105185343-kubernetes.md" >}}), specifically designed for [high-performance computing (HPC)]({{< relref "2025-09-08-123306-high_performance_computing_hpc.md" >}}) workloads. It addresses limitations within native Kubernetes for scenarios like machine learning, deep learning, big data, and scientific computing.
