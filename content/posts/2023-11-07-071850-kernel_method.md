kernel machines are a class of algorithms for [pattern recognition]({{< relref "2023-11-07-071454-pattern_recognition.md" >}}), whose best known member is the [support-vector machine (SVM)]({{< relref "2023-11-07-072035-support_vector_machine_svm.md" >}}). These methods involve using linear classifiers to solve nonlinear problems.

Kernel machines are slow to compute for datasets larger than a couple of thousand examples without parallel processing. The [nyström approximation]({{< relref "2023-11-07-072751-nystrom_approximation.md" >}}) can allow a significant speed-up of the computations.

Algorithms capable of operating with kernels include the kernel perceptron, support-vector machines (SVM), Gaussian processes, [Principal Component Analysis (PCA)]({{< relref "20230506180826-principal_component_analysis_pca.md" >}}), canonical correlation analysis, ridge regression, spectral clustering, linear adaptive filters and many others.
