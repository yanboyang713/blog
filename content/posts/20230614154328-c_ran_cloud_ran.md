C-RAN (Also CRAN) stands for Centralized RAN or Cloud RAN. In fact, I have seen the industry uses these terms interchangeably.


## Option 1 {#option-1}

In C-RAN (Cloud RAN) the [BBU]({{< relref "20230614155229-bbu.md" >}}) moves to a centralized location and the cell site only has the [antenna]({{< relref "20230614155634-antenna.md" >}}) and the [Radio Unit (RU)]({{< relref "20230614153814-ran_architecture.md#radio-unit--ru" >}}). This centralization of BBU functionality (also called BBU pool) results in the name centralized RAN or C-RAN.

{{< figure src="https://telcocloudbridge.com/wp-content/uploads/2021/04/image-1.png?ezimgfmt=ng:webp/ngcb1" >}}

This results in a new interface called [fronthaul]({{< relref "20230614195620-fronthaul.md" >}}), which is between the RRU and BBU pool.

The benefits of C-RAN includes CAPEX and OPEX reduction as deployment and maintenance cost per cell site are reduced because of [BBUs]({{< relref "20230614155229-bbu.md" >}}) centralization.

In addition, it improves spectral efficiency and reduces inter channel interferences, as centralized BBUs can share the resources dynamically among the multiple RRUs. The inter-channel interference is eased because of the joint scheduling and processing.


## Option 2: C-RAN with BBU Split {#option-2-c-ran-with-bbu-split}

In addition, a second option of the centralized RAN architecture has a further split in [BBUs]({{< relref "20230614155229-bbu.md" >}}) into [Distributed Unit (DU)]({{< relref "2024-10-28-235334-radio_access_network_ran_splits.md" >}}) and [CU]({{< relref "20230614200231-cu.md" >}}). Here, [CU]({{< relref "20230614200231-cu.md" >}}) is further towards the core network resulting in a new interface called [midhaul]({{< relref "20230614195820-midhaul.md" >}}).
