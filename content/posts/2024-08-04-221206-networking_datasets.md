## SNDlib {#sndlib}

Library with real-world network topologies (most popular: Abilene) and sometimes traffic/service demands: <http://sndlib.zib.de/>
Under Library &gt; Dynamic traffic there are realistic traffic traces that are dynamically changing over time. For example traffic matrix from every 5min over 6 months for the Abilene network.
Also for every 15min for the larger Geant network
