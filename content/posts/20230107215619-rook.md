## What is Rook? {#what-is-rook}

Rook is an open source cloud-native storage orchestrator for [Kubernetes]({{< relref "20230105185343-kubernetes.md" >}}), providing the platform, framework, and support for Ceph storage to natively integrate with Kubernetes.

[Ceph]({{< relref "20230107215132-ceph.md" >}}) is a distributed storage system that provides file, block and object storage and is deployed in large scale production clusters.

Rook automates deployment and management of Ceph to provide self-managing, self-scaling, and self-healing storage services. The Rook operator does this by building on Kubernetes resources to deploy, configure, provision, scale, upgrade, and monitor Ceph.
