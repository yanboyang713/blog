## Introduction {#introduction}

MikroTik is a networking vendor best known for [RouterOS]({{< relref "20230417083431-routeros.md" >}}) (their router/switch operating system) and RouterBOARD hardware. It’s commonly used for routing, switching, firewalling, VPNs, and wireless ([AP Controller (CAPsMAN)]({{< relref "2024-01-28-194857-ap_controller_capsman.md" >}})) in SOHO, labs, and small/medium ISP deployments.

-   Website: <https://mikrotik.com/>
-   RouterOS docs: <https://help.mikrotik.com/docs/display/ROS/RouterOS>
