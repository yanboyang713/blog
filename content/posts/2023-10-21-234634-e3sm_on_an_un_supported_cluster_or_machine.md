If you don't want to do a complete port of E3SM to your machine, you can use a "container" which has everything you need to develop and run.

We have development containers for [Docker]({{< relref "2023-10-21-235100-e3sm_on_docker.md" >}}) and [Singularity]({{< relref "2023-10-22-222431-e3sm_on_singularity.md" >}}).
